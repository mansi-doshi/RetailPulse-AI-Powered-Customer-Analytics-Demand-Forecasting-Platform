# 🛍️ RetailPulse – Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

📊 **RetailPulse – AI-Powered Customer Analytics & Demand Forecasting Platform** is a retail data analytics project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. It transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, key performance indicators (KPIs), and data visualizations.

The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, product trends, customer behaviour, revenue, profitability, and inventory risks to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📈 Analyze overall retail sales and revenue performance.
- 🏆 Identify top-performing products and brands.
- 👥 Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- 🌍 Compare sales across regions and time periods.
- 📦 Monitor inventory levels and potential stock risks.
- 📊 Develop interactive dashboards for business analysis.
- 💡 Support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- 📊 **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- 🧮 **DAX** – KPI calculations and analytical measures
- 📁 **CSV / Excel** – Data storage and preparation, where applicable
- 🐙 **GitHub** – Project hosting and documentation

- 💹 Total Profit
- 📦 Total Quantity Sold
- 🧾 Average Order Value
- 🏷️ Total Brands
- 🛍️ Total Products
- ⚠️ Inventory Risk Percentage
- 📅 Last Restock Date
- 📉 Low-Stock Products
